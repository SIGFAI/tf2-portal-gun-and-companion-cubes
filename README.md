# Portal Gun and Companion Cubes

**tf2** × Portal 2 · arme · idée de team

Give TF2 the real Portal gun: blue and orange portals on any wall, momentum kept through them like in Portal 2. Plus Companion Cubes you can pick up, carry and throw, just like the original.

> I gave TF2 a real Portal gun and throwable Companion Cubes. Falling into a floor portal and flying out a wall at 1400 speed never gets old.

## Installer

Copy the mod folder and the kit's game folder into Team Fortress 2's tf/custom folder, then start a bot match on koth_viaduct. Press 3 for the Portal Gun: click shoots a blue portal, right click an orange one, R grabs or drops a cube, and click while carrying throws it.
